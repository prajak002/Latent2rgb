The Entropy Ladder

Can the spectral entropy of a predicted latent — measured with no ground truth at all — warn that a frozen world model's rollout is degrading before the field's default proxy, latent-space distance, shows anything? We wire EBID's entropy-rate instability theory onto Horizon Ladder's real, instrumented V-JEPA2 rollout, and calibrate it against a second model family that has ground truth at every step.

01

The blind metric

Rolling a frozen latent-only predictor forward produces a state for a future that never happened. There's no image to decode it against, so nothing in the representation-inversion literature — Mahendran & Vedaldi, DPS, SPADE, Neuralangelo, GeoDE — can check it. The field's fallback is latent-space distance: cosine or L2 between predicted and some reference embedding.

Horizon Ladder's own Stage D result says that fallback is not sensitive to the thing it's supposed to measure:

Under a causal query the predictor was never trained for, latent_drift barely moves with horizon (2–3% from k=2→32) while pixel_error moves several times more, non-monotonically — on both datasets. latent2rgb/README.md, Stage D (pilot)

The metric the community reaches for when it has nothing else — latent distance — is close to flat exactly where the real failure is moving fastest. The open question is whether anything computable from the predicted latent alone, with no ground truth, tracks the real failure better.

02

EBID, in full

EBID (Entropy-Based Instability Dynamics) is the measurement layer of a broader framework, PCC, built around three non-transitively interacting forces:

drives change
Pressure

Amplification, optimization, the push toward a new state.

disrupts control
Chaos

Stochastic perturbation, unpredictability, injected noise.

suppresses pressure
Control

Stabilizes, regulates, pulls the system back toward consensus.

Control suppresses Pressure, Chaos disrupts Control, Pressure suppresses Chaos — a cyclic, rock–paper–scissors structure with no stable fixed point.

The measurable claim sits on top of a minimal dissipative extension of this system. Linearizing at the interior equilibrium gives a closed-form Hopf threshold:

Spectral threshold (pcc/RECOMMENDED_STRUCTURE.md)
λ±  =  (σ₀ − μ)  ±  i / √3
Hopf bifurcation at  σ₀ = μ
limit-cycle amplitude past threshold:  r* = √( (σ₀ − μ) / κ )

A quadratic expansion of Shannon entropy around that equilibrium shows entropy deficit scales with squared amplitude — which, combined with r* ∝ √(σ₀−μ), means entropy deficit is linear in distance past the instability threshold. That's the actual falsifiable claim: not just "entropy drops before a crash," but a specific scaling law, confirmed so far only on toy replicator populations and 2D spatial grids.

The transplant we're testing
toy system:  entropy_deficit  ∝  (σ₀ − μ)
this work:  entropy_deficit(ε)  ∝?  ε   — ε = controlled latent perturbation amplitude, playing the role of (σ₀−μ)
03

Two rollouts, one gap between them

The reason this is testable at all: two real autoregressive latent systems, one with ground truth at every step and one without.

Horizon Ladder — no ground truth

  • Frozen V-JEPA2 ViT-L encoder + predictor, causal query it was never trained for
  • Minimal linear decoder — deliberately weak, so it can't hallucinate away predictor failure
  • Stage F: decode → re-encode → re-inject; error shrinks with further horizon rather than compounding
  • Real data: Something-Something v2 + Kinetics-mini, both disjoint
latent2rgb (this repo)

Stream-DiffVSR — ground truth every step

  • Causal autoregressive latent diffusion, past frames only, 4-step distilled denoiser
  • ARTG: optical-flow-warped previous estimate fed as a ControlNet condition — a real, engineered anti-drift Control term
  • 720p in 0.328s (RTX4090), 130× latency reduction vs. TMP, LPIPS +0.095
  • Real HR target frame exists at every autoregressive step
arXiv:2512.23709 · github.com/jamichss/Stream-DiffVSR

Two-track design

Validate the entropy signal where you can check it against real pixel error (Track A), then trust it where you can't (Track B) — the design answers the obvious reviewer objection before it's asked.

06

Why this isn't just a metric with a new name

  • 1

    A scaling law, not a heuristic

    EBID's claim is entropy_deficit ∝ (σ₀−μ), a specific closed-form prediction from a Hopf bifurcation, not "entropy tends to drop before bad things." Part 3 tests whether that exact linear relationship survives the jump from a 3-strategy replicator to a real ViT-L latent rollout — falsifiable either way.

  • 2

    Calibrated before deployed

    Track A checks the entropy signal against real ground truth (Stream-DiffVSR has an HR frame every step). Track B applies the same, now-checked, signal where V-JEPA2 genuinely has none. Most entropy-as-diagnostic work skips the step where you prove the diagnostic means anything.

  • 3

    Decode as instrument, not deliverable

    The linear decoder is deliberately weak so it can't hallucinate away predictor failure — most evaluations use strong decoders that confound decoder skill with predictor skill. Already validated (Stage E: leakage and snapping checks both pass on real latents).

  • 4

    A real anti-drift mechanism to compare against

    ARTG is an engineered Control term in the PCC sense — motion-aligned conditioning that actively resists the same compounding drift Horizon Ladder measures passively. Ablating it (conditioning scale → 0) is a clean, already-built Pressure-vs-Control experiment.
