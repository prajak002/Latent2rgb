Field note — read before the images The predictor is being queried in a masking pattern it was never trained on (causal past→future, not the whole-clip completion it was pretrained for — see the interface report). Across this pilot, latent_drift moved only ~2% from k=2 to k=32 while pixel_error moved non‑monotonically by ~13%. These frames are the visual form of that flatness — watch whether the rolled-forward decode actually changes as the horizon grows, or whether it's converging on something horizon-independent.
