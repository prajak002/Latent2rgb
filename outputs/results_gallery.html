Horizon Ladder, V-JEPA2 diagnostic

Real footage → ground truth → rollout

How to read each card below: the model only ever sees stage ①. Stage ② is what actually happened next in the real video (the model never saw it). Stage ③ is the frozen model's prediction of what happens next, decoded to pixels. Stages ② and ③ are what get compared. Drag the handle.

Each card is also annotated with latent_drift and pixel_error's own range across k=2→32 — the numbers behind the pixels. They don't move by the same amount, and by how much varies clip to clip: this is the per-clip evidence for why no single latent-space proxy metric predicted pixel divergence across the six candidates tested in Experiments 1–6 (best correlation r = 0.12).
